# Welcome to DocuMind

DocuMind is a local-first, AI-powered document intelligence platform.

## What you can do

- Upload PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown files
- Ask natural-language questions about your documents
- Get answers grounded in your own content, with citations back to the
  exact filename, page, and chunk they came from
- Search across all of your documents semantically, not just by keyword

## How retrieval works

When you ask a question, DocuMind embeds it locally, searches your personal
vector index for the most relevant chunks, and passes only those chunks to
a locally running language model to generate a grounded answer. If nothing
relevant is found, DocuMind says so instead of guessing.
