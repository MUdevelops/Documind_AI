Q: Is my data sent to any third-party AI service?
A: No. Embeddings and language model inference both run locally on your
machine (sentence-transformers and Ollama respectively). Nothing leaves
your server unless you configure a remote provider yourself.

Q: What file types are supported?
A: PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown.

Q: What happens if a document fails to process?
A: Its status is marked "failed" with a specific error message (e.g.
unsupported format, empty file, corrupted file) -- it never silently
disappears or gets treated as processed.
